Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email epilogue.jw@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Why didn't some of my photos become pins?

Epilogue places pins using the location saved in each photo. Screenshots, saved images and photos taken with Location Services turned off for the Camera have no location. You can place those on the map by hand.

Why is a route shown as a straight dashed line?

Road routes are fetched the first time you're online after adding a stop. Until then, Epilogue shows a straight line between the stops.

Epilogue can't see my photos.

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Epilogue and choose Full Access, or use Select More Photos inside the app to add more.

How do I back up my trips?

Your trips are stored only on your iPhone. Use Settings › Export in the app to save a copy you can keep elsewhere.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Epilogue is built to keep your travels private. This policy explains what the app accesses and where that information goes.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips, pins, notes and stats are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Epilogue reads the location and date of the photos you choose. Your photos are never uploaded or copied off your device.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We don't run our own servers, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts.
  • We don't use our own analytics, advertising or tracking.

Maps and routes (Mapbox)

Epilogue uses Mapbox to show maps and to work out road routes between your stops. To do this, the app sends the following to Mapbox:

  • Requests for the map area you're viewing.
  • The coordinates of two consecutive stops when a road route is needed.

The Mapbox map also sends anonymous usage information, which may include your approximate position on the map, to help Mapbox improve its service.

None of this is linked to your identity. Mapbox's handling of this data is covered by the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

Your current location

If you allow it, Epilogue uses your current location only to centre the map on where you are. Epilogue doesn't store it or send it to us. The Mapbox map may include it in the anonymous usage information described above.

Sharing

When you share a trip or pin, you choose where it goes. By default, location data is removed from photos you share out of the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting a pin or trip in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all Epilogue data from your device.

Children

Epilogue isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
